🔵 Arousal

Both LUPI models outperform the baseline in accuracy and F1.

Representation alignment (α = 0.25) gives the strongest overall performance.

KL distillation shows more stable performance across α values.

Representation alignment collapses at α = 1, whereas KL remains stable.

Best Pearson correlation achieved with KL at α = 0.75.

Conclusion: Both mechanisms help arousal, with a slight edge to representation alignment at low α.

🔵 Valence

Representation alignment consistently outperforms both baseline and KL.

Best valence results achieved with representation alignment at α = 0.75.

KL distillation provides minimal or inconsistent improvements.

Representation alignment collapses at α = 1 (no ground-truth anchor).

Conclusion: Valence benefits more from internal representation alignment than from prediction distillation.

🔵 Comparative Takeaways

Representation alignment transfers richer information than KL.

KL is more stable but generally slightly weaker.

Moderate α values provide the best balance between ground truth and privileged guidance.

Internal feature guidance appears more beneficial than output-level distillation.
